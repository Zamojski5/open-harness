# Story check

Run on `01-treatment.md` before locking it, and again on `03-shot-list.md` (does the shot list still tell the story?). Even a 15 s ad needs a want, an obstacle and a turn.

## 1. Logline

One sentence: **who** (specific protagonist) **wants what**, **but** (obstacle/antagonist), **so/until** (the choice or turn). If you can't write it, the story isn't decided yet.

> A lighthouse keeper who has never left her island must row into the storm when the ship she warned ignores her signal.

## 2. Beats linked by causality

List the beats and join consecutive beats with **but** (complication) or **therefore** (consequence). If a link only works as **and then**, the beat is episodic — cut it, merge it, or rewrite it so it causes the next.

```
1. She lights the lamp every night — BUT tonight the lamp fails.
2. She repairs it by hand — THEREFORE she sees the ship too late to signal.
3. The ship turns toward the rocks — THEREFORE she takes the boat...
```

## 3. Climax by choice

The climax must be decided by the protagonist's **choice or action**, not by luck, an outside rescue or a reveal that just happens to them. Write the choice in one sentence; it should cost something.

## 4. Lengths

- Setup ≈ 20–25 %, confrontation ≈ 50–60 %, climax + resolution ≈ 15–25 % of runtime.
- Each scene gets a target length in seconds; total = target ±10 %.
- Rough shot budget: average shot 2–4 s (drama, AI shots 3–6 s), 1–2 s for action, 5–10 s for contemplative. A 60 s film ≈ 15–25 shots.
- Dialogue/VO: ~2.5 words/s EN, ~2.2 words/s PL; check every line fits its shot.

## 5. Rubric (score 1–5, one line of evidence each)

| Axis | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clarity | can't say what happens | understandable with the VO | clear with sound off |
| Want & stakes | no goal | goal stated | goal felt, loss is concrete |
| Causality | "and then" chain | mostly but/therefore | every beat causes the next |
| Climax by choice | happens to the hero | hero acts, little cost | hero chooses, at a cost |
| Visual storytelling | told in words | some key images | every beat has a picture |
| Fit to length & format | needs 3× the time | tight in places | fits with room to breathe |
| Feasibility with our tools | needs crowds, text, precise hands | some risky shots | every shot within tool strengths |
| Tone & ending | ending flat or contradicts tone | consistent | lands an emotion or idea |

**Any axis < 3 blocks the lock.** Fix the lowest axis first, re-score. **Max 3 revision rounds**; after that, present the treatment with the open weakness named and let the user decide (approve / change X / redo).

Feasibility tip: rewrite risky beats into strengths of the tools — silhouettes, wide shots, hands out of frame, environments, slow camera moves, single characters, objects with no readable text.
