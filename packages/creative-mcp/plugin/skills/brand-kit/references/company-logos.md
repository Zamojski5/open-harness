# Third-party company and product logos

For a real partner, integration, platform, sponsor, investor or competitor mark:

- Get the official file from the company's press/brand page (or `brand_capture` its site and take the header/nav mark, never a customer logo shown on their page). Prefer SVG; verify against a published hash when one is given.
- Use it unmodified, in a documented variant, at the size and clear space its guidelines ask for. Record source URL and date in `./sources.json`.
- A partner mark sits beside the user's identity; the design's identity stays the user's own brand, and never implies a partnership that doesn't exist.
- Check SVGs for live `<text>` (renders in a fallback font) — convert to paths or use the PNG.
