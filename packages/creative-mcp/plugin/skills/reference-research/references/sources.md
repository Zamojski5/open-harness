# Reference sources

What each source is good for, and how to get what you need from it. Interfaces change; if a page looks different, adapt and note it.

## Performance signals

| Source                                       | What it tells you                                                                                                                               | Tips                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Meta Ad Library (facebook.com/ads/library)   | Which ads a brand is running, since when. An ad live for many weeks, or one of many variants of the same concept, is usually paying for itself. | Search by brand or keyword, filter country and media type "video", sort by longest running. Note start date, number of ad variants, placements, CTA. Competitors' evergreen ads matter more than new ones. |
| TikTok Creative Center, Top Ads              | Ranked ads by industry, country, objective and period, with CTR/CVR bands and 6s view rate.                                                     | Filter to the category and the audience's country; watch the top ones and the fast risers. Likes alone are a weak signal; look at the engagement tiers it shows.                                           |
| Google Ads Transparency Center               | All ads a verified advertiser runs on Google and YouTube, with regions and formats.                                                             | Good for competitor video ads and last-shown dates.                                                                                                                                                        |
| LinkedIn Ad Library                          | B2B ads by company or keyword.                                                                                                                  | Useful for B2B and employer brand. Only copy and format are visible, not spend.                                                                                                                            |
| YouTube                                      | Views against channel size, comments, retention hints in the chapters, ads seen via search.                                                     | A video far above the channel's usual views is the signal, not the raw number.                                                                                                                             |
| Top organic shorts and reels in the category | What the audience watches when nobody pays for it.                                                                                              | Search the category hashtag or phrase and sort by popular; compare creators, not only brands.                                                                                                              |

## Craft archives

Ads of the World, Cannes Lions, D&AD, The One Show, Motionographer, Behance and Vimeo staff picks, and the brands' own channels. They show quality and invention, not business results, so label them "craft only" unless a result is stated.

## Branding range

For how a brand flexes its system: the brand's social feed scrolled over several months, its campaign pages, case studies from its agencies, and the Wayback Machine for earlier campaigns. For the client: their site sections, past key visuals, packaging, decks, photo and illustration libraries.

## Practicalities

- `reference_download` takes any URL yt-dlp supports. If it says no video was found or the page needs login, find the same ad on YouTube or the brand's own page, or ask the user for the file. Do not bypass logins or paywalls.
- Record the date you looked: library results and rankings change.
- Capture the signal in library.json as a plain fact ("running since 2026-07-02, 14 variants") so patterns.md can cite it.
